01 — HEART HEALTH

Understanding high blood pressure

The WHO defines hypertension in adults as blood pressure of 140/90 mmHg or higher, confirmed on measurements taken on two different days. Many people have no symptoms, so only a health professional can assess readings and diagnose the condition.

WHO: Hypertension ↗
02 — CHRONIC CONDITIONS

Learning about diabetes

Diabetes occurs when the pancreas does not produce enough insulin or the body cannot use it effectively. Diagnosis and care require individual assessment by a qualified health professional; this summary is not a treatment plan.

WHO: Diabetes ↗
03 — DENGUE

Recognizing dengue warning signs

Dengue is transmitted by infected mosquitoes. Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, rapid breathing, bleeding gums or nose, blood in vomit or stool, unusual tiredness, or cold and pale skin can signal severe dengue; seek urgent medical care if these occur.
